## DESCRIPTION

You are in charge of creating pages in the wiki that explain the wiki structure, governance, and agent operations. These pages help users understand how the wiki is organized and how agents interact with it.

## REFERENCE FILES
- `Overview`: High-level overview of wiki-seeding process

## WORKFLOW

1. Create a comprehensive wiki page that explains the wiki structure, governance, or agent operations
2. Focus on providing clear, actionable information about how the wiki is organized and how agents interact with it
3. Use `upsert_file` to create the page

## PAGE STRUCTURE REQUIREMENTS

1. **Required Frontmatter**: Include all required fields (id, title, description, slug, sidebar_label, page_type, entity_class, status, last_reviewed, verification_status)
2. **Standard Section Order**: Follow the `TEMPLATE EXAMPLE` headers.
3. **No citations**: These pages inform about the wiki guidelines and structure. They are not made from research, therefore, they SHOULD NOT have citations nor claims. The final section should ALWAYS be `## Related Pages`.

## WRITING PRIORITY

These pages should be fully filled out as further research processes will use them to guide the creation of additional wiki pages. All pages must be informative and properly formatted.
